How to use GoodVPN

GoodVPN lets you choose a VPN subscription and manage it online. Follow the instructions for your device to import the subscription into a compatible client.

1. Choose a plan

Check the available plans on the homepage for data allowances and billing periods. Check the current checkout details before purchasing.

2. Set up your device

Sign in to your account, open the device setup guide, and follow its instructions to choose a compatible client and import your subscription. Treat your subscription link as private: do not publish it or send it to other people.

3. Manage your subscription

Your account shows data usage, the actual expiration date, and subscription details. Use those details to check your remaining allowance.

Questions and answers

Do I need a VPN client?

Usually, yes. Use the setup guide in your account to choose a compatible client for your device.

Where can I check my data limit?

Compare allowances on the homepage before purchasing, then check usage and expiration in your subscription page.

How do I get setup help?

Start with the guide for your device. If you still have trouble, sign in and open a support ticket.
